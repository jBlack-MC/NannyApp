1. Product vision and scope

Nanny-App is a childcare marketplace that helps parents discover verified nannies, arrange care, coordinate the session and maintain a transparent manual payment ledger. It gives nannies tools to present their experience, publish availability, manage work and build reputation. Web administrators verify providers, moderate users, resolve disputes, support customers and reconcile payment records.

Product boundary. The pilot records held, released and refunded payment states; it does not charge cards, transfer money or provide automated escrow. Android supports parent and nanny journeys. Administrative and maintenance work remains web-only.

4. User stories and acceptance criteria

Parent

  • As a parent, I can book eligible care. Given a verified account, owned child, future time within published availability and no overlap, creation stores the booking and agreed amount; invalid ownership, time or conflict returns a safe error.
  • As a parent, I can verify arrival. A six-digit PIN is visible only to the owning parent; the assigned nanny can use it once, with five failed attempts before regeneration is required.
  • As a parent, I can resolve completion. After checkout, confirmation completes the booking and releases the held ledger entry atomically; a dispute preserves the ledger for admin review.

Nanny

  • As a nanny, I can manage availability. Valid non-overlapping daily time ranges save; bookings must fit every requested minute.
  • As a nanny, I can manage assigned work. Only the assigned nanny may accept, reject, check in or check out from allowed states.

Administrator

  • As an admin, I can triage risk. The action centre exposes counts and links for safety tickets, disputes, verification, receipts, contacts and worker queues.
  • As an admin, I can reconcile manually. Actions require CSRF protection and role checks and explicitly state that no bank transfer occurs.

6. Scope and release gates

Included: web/PWA, native Android, shared API/database, local or S3-compatible private storage, account email, manual ledger and web administration.

Excluded from pilot: native iOS, automatic card charging, automatic payouts, bank escrow, public document storage and multi-region high availability.

Launch gates: production domain and HTTPS, secrets, migrations, worker scheduling, verified email delivery, signed APK, private upload backup, restore drill, real-device/accessibility checks, policy content and operational ownership.
